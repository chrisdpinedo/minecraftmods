FLUSHABLE TOILET MOD - HOW TO INSTALL
=====================================

You need: Minecraft Java Edition on a Windows PC.
(It won't work on Bedrock Edition: Xbox, Switch, phone, or the
Windows Bedrock version.)

You should have received this file:
    FlushableToilet-Installer-26.3.zip


INSTALLING
----------
1. Right-click the zip and choose "Extract All".
   Don't run anything from inside the zip - the installer needs the
   "files" folder next to it.

2. Close the Minecraft Launcher completely.

3. In the extracted folder, double-click "install.bat".
   If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen,
   click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   A window shows the progress. When it says "Done!", close it.

4. Open the Minecraft Launcher. Next to the Play button, pick
   "Fabric 26.3 (Flushable Toilet)".

5. Press Play. The first launch takes a minute or two while it
   downloads Minecraft 26.3.


CHECKING IT WORKED
------------------
In Creative mode, look for the "Toilet" in the Functional Blocks tab.

In Survival, craft it with 4 Blocks of Quartz and a Water Bucket
(you get the bucket back):

    .  .  Q        Q = Block of Quartz
    Q  W  Q        W = Water Bucket
    .  Q  .

Right-click the toilet to flush it. Anything dropped in the bowl gets
flushed away - don't drop anything you want to keep!


PLAYING TOGETHER
----------------
Everyone who plays needs the mod installed.

1. One person opens a world, presses Esc, and chooses "Open to LAN".
2. Everyone else goes to Multiplayer. The world shows up there to join.
