FLUSHABLE TOILET - Minecraft 26.3 mod

Install:
  1. Extract this whole zip (right-click > Extract All).
  2. Close the Minecraft Launcher.
  3. Double-click install.bat
     (or in PowerShell: powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\install.ps1)
  4. Open the Minecraft Launcher, choose "Fabric 26.3 (Flushable Toilet)"
     next to the Play button, and press Play.

Playing together: everyone who joins needs this installed.
One person opens a world, presses Esc > Open to LAN, and the others
join from Multiplayer.

Using the toilet: right-click to flush. Items dropped in the bowl get
flushed away. Craft it with 4 Blocks of Quartz and a Water Bucket:
    . . Q
    Q W Q
    . Q .

Included: Flushable Toilet 1.1.0, Fabric API 0.164.0, Fabric Loader 0.19.5.
